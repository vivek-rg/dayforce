# Documents required for onboarding
Every new hire submits a government-issued identity document and a signed employment letter before the start date. Employees also submit address proof, educational certificates and previous employment documents where they apply.

# Bank and tax forms
Employees submit bank details and tax forms to payroll through the document portal. Contractors invoice the company and do not submit these forms.

# How documents are verified
A member of HR reviews every document. AI suggestions are advisory, and only a person can mark a document verified. If a document is rejected, HR gives a reason and you can upload a new version.
