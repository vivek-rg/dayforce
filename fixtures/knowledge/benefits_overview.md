# Benefits available
Full-time employees are offered group medical insurance, life insurance and a wellness allowance of 12,000 rupees per year. Contractors are not eligible for company benefits.

# How to enrol
You will receive an invitation from the benefits team in your first week. Choose to enrol or decline within 30 days of your start date.

# Dependants
Medical insurance can include a spouse and up to two children. Add dependants during your enrolment window.
