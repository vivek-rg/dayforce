# How to request a laptop
Your manager or IT raises an equipment request during onboarding. IT approves a specific device at your work location. A reservation means the device is set aside for you; it does not mean you have received it.

# Collecting your device
Office employees collect their laptop from IT on the first day and sign the handover. Remote employees receive a shipped device, and handover is recorded on delivery.

# Lost or damaged equipment
Report lost or damaged equipment to IT within 24 hours so that it can be secured.
