# How to claim reimbursement
Submit an expense claim with the original receipt to your manager within 30 days of the expense. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

# Travel expenses
Economy class air and rail travel is reimbursed for approved business trips. Local taxi fares are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day.

# Home office allowance
Remote employees can claim a one-time home office allowance of up to 10,000 rupees for a desk or chair, with receipts.
