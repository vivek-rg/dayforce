# Work from home
Office-based employees may work from home up to two days per week with their manager's agreement. Remote employees work from home by default.

# Remote work requirements
Remote work requires a reliable internet connection and use of the company VPN for internal systems.

# Working from another city
Tell HR before working from another city for longer than two weeks, because your official work location may need to change.
